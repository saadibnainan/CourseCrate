# CourseCrate privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This policy covers CourseCrate for Firefox and for Chrome (and other Chromium browsers). Both versions are the same code.

CourseCrate does not collect, send or sell any data. There is no analytics, no tracking, no account and no server.

**What it reads.** When you click the CourseCrate button on a Moodle page, it reads that Moodle site's pages and files using the login you already have in your browser, exactly as your browser does when you click around the course. It only talks to Moodle sites you have allowed (the browser asks once per site), and nothing else.

**What it stores, on your computer only** (the extension's local storage):

- your CourseCrate settings;
- for sync mode, the list of file addresses already saved from each course and where they were saved;
- the last 12 courses you opened in CourseCrate (site, course id, course name), shown on the start page.

You can clear the sync history from the settings page. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

**What it saves.** Course files go to your Downloads folder through your browser's download manager.

**How this data is used.** Only to show and run your downloads. It is never transferred, sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising, credit or anything unrelated to downloading your course.

**Moodle's own logs.** Your Moodle site records page visits as usual, so CourseCrate opening an activity looks the same to Moodle as you opening it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/saadibnainan/CourseCrate/issues.
