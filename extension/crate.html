Open a Moodle course, then click CourseCrate.

CourseCrate saves a whole course to your computer: every file, folder, assignment brief and forum attachment, plus a page of links, sorted into one folder per section.

  1. 1
    Log in to Moodle in this browser and open any page of the course.
  2. 2
    Click the CourseCrate button in the toolbar. If it's not there, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin it.
  3. 3
    Pick what you want and press Download. Run it again later and only new files come down.
